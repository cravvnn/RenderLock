RenderLock — replay performances in Blender

1. Extract the ENTIRE Windows ZIP, then open RenderLock.exe.
   Launch_RenderLock.cmd is the fallback launcher. Python, the converter and
   helper tools are included; install Blender separately.
2. In Setup, choose Blender and your Deadlock installation.
3. In Export, inspect a .dem, select characters and start with a short tick range.
   Use Standard materials and turn off optional effects/cloth/map for the first
   animation check. Keep broad partial export off for full animation.
4. Choose a new .blend output, export, then read the coverage report.

Optional features:
- Map: Download map, extract it, choose RenderLockMap.blend and enable inclusion.
  RenderLock appends and aligns it. Relink Textures and cut the final map manually.
- Lighting lab: Source lighting and Elagrimm + Source are partial character looks.
  The Elagrimm library is a separate download. Accept lighting limits explicitly.
- Advanced: supported cloth, particles, model bone rules and material repairs.
  Cloth-only, lighting-only and broad partial-character choices have different scopes.

Keep the .blend, .agrscene DIRECTORY and .agr-report.json together. Map textures
remain external until relinked/packed. Read README.md and docs/README.md for guides.

For a stopped export, review the recovery choices and exact diagnostic path.
Logs/support ZIPs normally go to %LOCALAPPDATA%\RenderLock\logs. Closing recovery
never retries. Review diagnostics before sharing paths or replay information.
